Trust, Safety & Fair Access

How ResourceBridge bridges the gap between idle college assets and ambitious student builders.

🎯

The Challenge in Higher Education

College departments invest heavily in specialized resources: electron microscopes, cinematic camera packages, high-end GPU workstations, audio booths, and laser cutters.

However, these assets are often locked in departmental silos. After 4:00 PM, an estimated 65% to 80% of specialized college facilities sit dark and unutilized, while students working on capstones or startup prototypes lack access.

🛡️

The Four Pillars of Trust

  • 1. Verified Campus Membership: Every participant has an authenticated identity tied to an active institutional status.
  • 2. Controlled Role Permissions: Strict segregation of duties. Students request, Managers inspect and approve, Admins set governance rules.
  • 3. Check-out / Check-in Accountability: Clear audit trails recording who took what, when, and the logged condition upon return.
  • 4. Anti-Hoarding Rules: Fair access time caps and active reservation limits prevent any one group from monopolizing shared gear.
⚡

Architecture & Tech Stack

Built with clean, maintainable, dependency-conscious Python technology:

Backend: Python 3 + Flask Application Factory
Database: SQLite with Flask-SQLAlchemy ORM
Session & Auth: Flask-Login + Werkzeug Password Hashing
Frontend: Jinja2 HTML5 Templates, Vanilla CSS & Vanilla JS
